## Full description (paste this into YouTube)

GoalMuse turns your vision board and daily habits into one clear next step and a progress score you can trust. This demo walks through the app: vision boards, goals, tasks, journal, and AI-powered insights.

In this video you'll see:
• Sign in and create a vision board with goals
• Add tasks and a journal entry (linked to goals)
• Progress confidence score (0–100) with a transparent breakdown—not just task count
• Three AI agents: Alignment (actions vs goals), Synergy (connections between goals, compound actions), Optimization (one recommended next action with a reason)
• "Did this help?" feedback and a quick account flow

GoalMuse is a mobile app (Expo/React Native) with a FastAPI backend and explainable AI. We use Opik for observability so every improvement is measurable.
